Governance & Transparency

Privacy Policy & Terms

How Brainz Edu World protects personal data, ensures student safety, and maintains ethical digital standards across our community.

Last Updated: September 2026 | Applicable to Students, Parents & Visitors

1 Overview & Institutional Commitment

Brainz Edu World, Meerut ("School", "We", "Our") is dedicated to upholding the highest standards of integrity, safety, and confidentiality in handling information provided by prospective and enrolled students, parents, guardians, and visitors.

This document explains our principles regarding the collection, processing, and protection of information obtained through our official website (brainzeduworld.com), admission inquiry portals, school management platforms, and campus interactions.

2 Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to fulfill our educational, statutory, and administrative functions. Categories of data include:

  • Admission & Inquiry Information: Student name, date of birth, previous school attended, grade sought, parent/guardian contact numbers, residential address, and email address submitted via website inquiry forms.
  • Academic & Administrative Records: Attendance data, report cards, CBSE board registration details, and emergency medical contacts required for enrolled students.
  • Payment & Billing Data: Fee payment records processed via our authorized payment and student management partner portal (MyClassBoard / MCB). We do not store credit/debit card numbers or net banking credentials on our local servers.
  • Website Usage & Diagnostics: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic data such as browser type, operating system, and pages visited to ensure optimal performance.

3 How We Use Your Information

Data gathered by Brainz Edu World is used strictly for legitimate educational and institutional activities:

  • Evaluating admissions eligibility, conducting entrance interactions, and communicating admission status.
  • Sending official circulars, academic progress reports, transport updates, and emergency alerts.
  • Meeting regulatory reporting standards set forth by the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) and Department of Education, Uttar Pradesh.
  • Enhancing campus safety and providing emergency medical attention when required.
Strict No-Spam & Non-Commercial Policy: Brainz Edu World never sells, rents, trades, or commercially exploits personal information belonging to parents, students, or staff with any third-party marketing agency.

4 Student Media & Campus Photography Policy

As an active institution with vibrant celebrations, sports leagues, exhibitions, and STEAM achievements, the school routinely documents campus life through photography and video recordings.

  • School life images published on our website, social media pages, and annual newsletters showcase collaborative student accomplishments and educational milestones.
  • Photographs are curated ethically. Full home addresses, sensitive medical notes, or unauthorized personal contact details of minors are never published alongside media.
  • Parents and legal guardians who prefer that their ward's photograph not be included in public promotional media may submit a written notice to the school administrative office at the commencement of each academic year.

5 Terms of Service & Website Usage

By accessing or interacting with brainzeduworld.com and affiliated web applications, you agree to comply with the following institutional guidelines:

  • Intellectual Property: All text, campus photographs, educational curricula, school crest, logos, and digital assets published on this site are the exclusive intellectual property of Brainz Edu World. Reproduction without written consent is prohibited.
  • Accurate Submissions: Parents and guardians submitting admission inquiries or scholarship applications agree to provide truthful and accurate information.
  • Portal Access & Conduct: Users granted login credentials for fee portals or learning management platforms must protect their passwords and avoid unauthorized access attempts.
  • AI Assistant (Brainzly): The Brainzly AI assistant provides general guidance on school schedules, admissions, and facilities. Official admissions confirmations and fee schedules are governed by formal school administrative circulars.

6 Data Security & Retention

We deploy robust administrative, physical, and technical security protocols to protect school records from unauthorized access, accidental alteration, or disclosure. Physical records are retained in restricted administrative archives, and digital databases employ encrypted connections (HTTPS / TLS).

Student records are retained for periods required to fulfill educational accreditations, CBSE board verification, and legal compliances.
